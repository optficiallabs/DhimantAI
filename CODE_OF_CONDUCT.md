# Contributor Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, professional, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in DhimantAI.

Expected behaviour includes respectful communication, constructive technical discussion, privacy awareness, and responsible handling of education-related information.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, deliberate disruption, or misuse of project channels.

Because DhimantAI relates to education, contributors must not share identifiable student data, confidential institution information, restricted assessment material, credentials, or other sensitive content.

Concerns may be reported privately to `contact@optficial.ai`.
